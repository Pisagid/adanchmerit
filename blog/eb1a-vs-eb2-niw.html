EB-1A or EB-2 NIW: which should you choose?

Updated 16 November 2026 · Adanch Merit Consulting

Short answer: EB-2 NIW suits more people. It asks for an advanced degree and work of national importance. EB-1A is for people at the very top of their field. It is faster for Nigerians and needs no degree, but the bar is higher. A free check tells you which one fits your record.

The EB-1A criteria

To pass the first step of EB-1A you need a one-time major international award (think Nobel-level) or evidence of at least 3 of these 10:

  • Nationally or internationally recognised prizes or awards
  • Membership in associations that require outstanding achievement
  • Published material about you in major media or trade publications
  • Judging the work of others (for example, peer review or competition panels)
  • Original contributions of major significance
  • Authorship of scholarly articles
  • Display of your work at exhibitions or showcases
  • A leading or critical role for distinguished organisations
  • A high salary compared with others in your field
  • Commercial success in the performing arts

Meeting three is only the start. USCIS then looks at the whole record and asks whether you have sustained national or international acclaim and are among the small percentage at the top of your field. Many petitions fail at this second step.

How to choose

Lean towards EB-2 NIW if…

  • You have a Master's, PhD, or a Bachelor's with 5+ years of progressive experience.
  • Your work clearly helps the US (health, energy, infrastructure, tech, education, research), but you're not yet famous in your field.
  • You can show a strong, specific plan for your work in the US.

Lean towards EB-1A if…

  • You can clearly prove at least 3 criteria with strong documents, not just claims.
  • Independent experts outside your circle know your work.
  • You've won recognised awards, been featured in major media, judged others' work, or led well-known organisations.
  • You don't have an advanced degree but have an outstanding record, for example in business, music, film, fashion or sport.
