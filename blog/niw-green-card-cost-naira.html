How much does an EB-2 NIW green card cost in 2026, in dollars and naira?

Updated 16 November 2026 · Adanch Merit Consulting

Short answer: for one person applying from Nigeria, the US government fees for an EB-2 NIW green card add up to about $1,795–$2,095, roughly ₦2,423,000–₦2,828,000. Premium processing adds $2,965 (about ₦4,003,000). Consulting or attorney fees are separate.

Exchange rate used: ₦1,350 per $1, close to the official NFEM rate on 10 November 2026 (₦1,350.86). Street (parallel market) rates were higher, around ₦1,410–₦1,430. Naira figures are rounded to the nearest ₦1,000 and will move with the rate. Always check the day's rate before you pay.

Costs that aren't in these tables

  • Professional help: consulting and attorney fees vary a lot by how much work your evidence needs. We quote after a free check.
  • Documents: international passports, police certificates, birth and marriage certificates, translations and courier fees.
  • Evidence costs: credential evaluation, certified copies, and sometimes expert opinion letters.
  • Travel: getting to Abuja or Lagos for the medical and interview, then flights to the US.
  • Bank charges: card FX fees and the gap between official and bank rates.

Does EB-1A cost the same?

Yes, mostly. EB-1A uses the same Form I-140, the same $1,015 petition total and the same $2,965 premium processing fee (for a 15-business-day decision), and the same consular fees afterwards. The difference is usually in how much evidence work your case needs.

Ways to keep costs down

  • Start gathering civil documents early, so you're not paying rush fees later.
  • Only pay for premium processing when the timing makes a real difference.
  • Get an honest eligibility check before you spend on letters and evidence. A weak case filed early can cost more than a strong one filed later.
