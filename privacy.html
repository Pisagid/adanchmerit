Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to assess your green card options and reply to you. We never sell your data. You can ask us to see, correct or delete it at any time by emailing adanchmerit@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Adanch Merit Consulting ("we", "us") helps professionals prepare EB-1A and EB-2 National Interest Waiver green card petitions. We are a consulting service, not a law firm. We decide how and why your personal data is used, so we are responsible for it.

Contact: adanchmerit@gmail.com · WhatsApp +234 707 040 0146

2. What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, email, WhatsApp number, country, field of work, qualifications, achievements, questions, and any CV or documents you send.
  • Self-check answers: if you send your result from our eligibility check, the answers you chose.
  • Client files: if you become a client, the evidence you share for your petition, such as degrees, letters, publications, awards and employment records.
  • Messages: what you write to us on WhatsApp, email or social media.

Please do not send passport numbers, bank details or other identity numbers through the website form. If we need them later, we will ask for them in a safer way.

PurposeLegal basis

4. Who we share it with

We share your data only when needed, and never sell it.

  • US immigration attorneys: only with your permission, when you ask us to introduce a partner attorney to review your case.
  • Service providers that help us run the service: FormSubmit (forwards website form messages to our email), Google (email and files), Meta (WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram messages) and GitHub (hosts this website).
  • Authorities: only if the law requires it.

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria, mainly in the United States. We use well-known providers with their own security and data-protection commitments.

5. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become clients: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Client files: for as long as your case needs, then up to 6 years for our records, unless you ask us to delete them sooner and the law allows it.

6. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and similar laws where you live, you can:

  • ask for a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • withdraw your consent, or object to how we use it;
  • ask us to move it to someone else; and
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), or the data protection authority in your country.

To use any of these rights, email adanchmerit@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

7. Security

We limit who can see your data, use accounts protected by strong passwords and two-step sign-in, and delete files we no longer need. No method is perfectly secure, so please tell us at once if you think your data has been exposed.

8. Cookies and this website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It saves two small settings in your own browser: whether you have read our opening notice, and whether you chose light or dark mode. They never leave your device. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this page first.

9. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, except details of a client's children that are needed for their family's petition, shared by that client.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell clients about important changes.
