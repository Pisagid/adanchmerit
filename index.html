How the NIW compares to other US routes

  • You apply on your own. No US employer has to sponsor you.
  • It rewards what you have already built: degrees, results, impact.
  • Leads straight to permanent residency, not a temporary visa.
  • You can file from Nigeria. Your spouse and children under 21 can usually come too.
  • A green card is the path to US citizenship later.
H-1B work visa

Needs an employer, an annual lottery and is temporary.

EB-2 / EB-3 with PERM

Needs a US job offer and labour certification before you can even file.

EB-1A extraordinary ability

No employer needed, but the bar is the very top of your field. Approvals fell to about 32% in mid-2026.

EB-5 investor

Requires an investment of $800,000 or more.

F-1 study visa

Temporary, with limited work rights after graduation.

Why now

The National Interest Waiver is open to you, but USCIS is reviewing petitions more strictly than ever.

≈ 1 in 2

NIW petitions decided from October 2025 to June 2026 were denied, mostly because the evidence did not prove one of the three tests.

Since 5 August 2026, USCIS can deny a petition with missing evidence without warning (Policy Alert PA-2026-05). Your first filing has to be complete.

Evidence that builds your case

USCIS has no list of approved jobs. It looks at proof. These are the five kinds we help you gather and present.

Leadership & critical roles

Leadership & critical roles

Projects you led, teams you managed, and results you can measure: cost saved, users reached, lives improved. Letters from managers and clients make this count.

Publications & citations

Publications & citations

Papers, reports, conference talks and how often others cite or use them. Not essential for everyone, but strong proof of influence when you have them.

Original contributions

Original contributions

Products, methods, patents or systems you created that others now use. Show adoption: who uses it, where, and what changed because of it.

Recognition & media

Recognition & media

Awards, press coverage, invited talks, judging others' work, and membership of professional bodies such as NSE, COREN or the NMA.

Your US plan

Your US plan

A clear "proposed endeavor": what you will do in the US and why it matters nationally. Backed by interest from employers, clients, investors or partners.

    FAQs

    What is Adanch Merit Consulting?

    A consulting service that helps Nigerian and African professionals prepare complete, evidence-backed EB-2 National Interest Waiver petitions for a US green card.

    What is the EB-2 NIW?

    A US green card route for people with an advanced degree or exceptional ability. It waives the job offer and labour certification, so you can petition for yourself.

    Who qualifies?

    You need a master's degree or higher, or a bachelor's plus five years of progressive experience, or exceptional ability. Then you must show your US work has national importance and that you are well placed to do it.

    Do I need a PhD or publications?

    No. They help, but what matters most is the impact of your work and how well you prove it.

    Can I apply while living in Nigeria?

    Yes. After the petition is approved and a visa number is available, your case moves to the National Visa Center and an interview at the US Embassy in Abuja or the Consulate in Lagos.

    Can my family come with me?

    Your spouse and unmarried children under 21 can usually get green cards as your dependants on the same case.

    How much do your services cost?

    We quote after your free eligibility check, based on how much evidence work your case needs. US government fees are separate and listed above.

    How long does it take?

    The petition can take over a year on standard processing, or about 45 business days with premium processing. After approval, timing depends on your country of birth and the monthly Visa Bulletin.

    Are you a law firm?

    No. We do not give legal advice or represent you before USCIS. We recommend review by a licensed US immigration attorney before filing and can introduce one.

    Can you guarantee approval?

    No one can. USCIS alone decides every case. Our job is to make your petition as complete and well evidenced as possible.
